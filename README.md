# Hi there 👋

I’m a PhD candidate in High-Energy Physics working on real-time machine learning systems for nanosecond-scale decision making. My research focuses on [FPGA-optimized neural architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12850), [hardware-native online learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02056), and [large-scale statistical analyses for precision Higgs measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05362).
Recently, I’ve been looking at extending these techniques toward [quantum–classical integration for scalable quantum technologies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02005).

Beyond research, having grown up in Central Vietnam, I’m deeply committed to expanding access to technology education in underserved communities. I currently lead SEAS — Summer in Engineering and Applied Sciences, an initiative aimed at improving STEM education access in Central Vietnam:
👉 https://seas-cvn.com/
